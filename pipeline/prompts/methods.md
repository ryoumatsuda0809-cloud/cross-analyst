TASK: Identify the diagnostic methods described in the attached research papers.

You are reading the method sections of one or more academic papers. Turn each distinct method into a
"method card" that someone could later apply to a company's financial statements.

You have NOT been given any company data, on purpose. Describe each method as the paper states it.
Do not adapt it to any particular company.

For each method, write:

## M<number>: <short name of the method>

- **Source:** the attachment file name and the paper title
- **What it diagnoses:** one sentence
- **Inputs required:** the financial line items or ratios the method needs, using plain names
  (e.g. current_assets, current_liabilities, operating_cash_flow, net_income, total_assets)
- **Procedure:** the steps or formula, exactly as the paper gives them
- **Thresholds / interpretation:** the cut-off values and what they mean. Quote the paper's own words
  in quotation marks for every threshold. If the paper gives no threshold, write "Not stated in the paper."
- **Limitations stated by the authors:** quote them, or write "Not stated in the paper."

Rules:
1. Use only what is in the attachments. Never fill a gap from general knowledge; write "Not stated in the paper."
2. Never invent a threshold. A threshold without a quotation is not allowed.
3. If two papers describe the same method, make one card and list both sources.
4. Number the cards M1, M2, ... in order.
