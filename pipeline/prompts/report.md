TASK: Write the final financial diagnostic report in Markdown.

Attachments: the method cards (02_methods.md), the computed metrics (03a_metrics.md) and the
diagnosis (03_diagnosis.md). The reader is the company's owner, who is not a finance specialist.

Structure:

# Financial Diagnostic Report: <company name from 03a_metrics.md>

## Summary
Three to five bullet points: the most important findings, each tagged with its method ID.

## Findings
For each finding: what it means in plain words, the evidence (numbers copied from 03a_metrics.md
with metric name and period), the method ID, and the confidence level.

## Where the methods disagree
Copy the substance from the diagnosis. Do not resolve the disagreement.

## Questions to verify with the company
The questions from "What the data cannot tell us", rewritten so an owner can answer them.

## Methods used
One line per method card: ID, name, source paper. Mark methods that were not applicable and why.

## Limitations
What this report cannot conclude, including the authors' stated limitations.

Rules:
1. Every number must appear in 03a_metrics.md exactly as written there. No new calculations.
2. Every finding names its method ID.
3. Hypotheses, not verdicts. Do not recommend specific products, loans or services.
4. Do not include anything that is not supported by the attachments.
